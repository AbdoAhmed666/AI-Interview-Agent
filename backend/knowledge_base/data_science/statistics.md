# Statistics for data science

## Descriptive statistics

Mean, median, and mode summarize central tendency; variance and standard
deviation summarize spread. The median is robust to outliers, the mean is not.

## Distributions

The normal distribution underlies many methods. The central limit theorem says
sample means approach a normal distribution as sample size grows, which
justifies many confidence intervals and tests.

## Inference and hypothesis testing

A p-value is the probability of observing data at least as extreme as the sample
under the null hypothesis. A small p-value (typically < 0.05) suggests the effect
is unlikely under the null. Confidence intervals communicate uncertainty better
than a single point estimate. Beware multiple-comparison problems.

## Correlation vs causation

Correlation measures linear association and does not imply causation. Confounding
variables can create spurious correlations. Controlled experiments (A/B tests)
or careful causal inference are needed to claim cause and effect.
